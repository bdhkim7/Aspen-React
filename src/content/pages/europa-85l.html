Europa85L

Built for Portability & Performance

Full-unit performance meets portable versatility

The Europa85L is an ultra-portable X-ray system engineered for environments where mobility, flexibility, and efficient workflow matter most. Built with a lightweight form factor and advanced power efficiency, Europa85L supports extremity image acquisition in portable imaging settings, with images viewed and managed through an external workstation or PACS for seamless integration
  • Ultra-lightweight handheld X-ray device with clinical-grade imaging performance
  • Fast capture workflow with external PC image viewing
  • Battery-powered operation for complete portability

  • ideal for mobile use and space-limited environments
External Workstation Workflow
  • Operate with your preferred laptop or medical workstation

Meet Europa85L

Key Features

Europa85L

Highlights

Portable, Lightweight Construction

Weighing only 8 lb, Europa85L makes it easy to manuever between patient rooms, mobile setups or temporary locations.
Its compact form factor allow for simple positioning during extremity radiographic procedures

External Workstation Integration

Europa85L operates with an external workstation. Image capture, processing, and DICOM transfers are all handled entirely by the workstation's DR software, which supports flexible configuration options

Reliable X-ray Output

Europa85L provides consistent generator performance for extremity radiographic imaging with:

Adjustable 50-85kV
2-5mA range
0.8mm focal spot
0.03-2.0 second exposure time

Designed for Space-Limited Use


Its compact design allows Europa85L to be a perfect fit for:

Small imaging rooms
Mobile carts
Temporary care areas

Compatible with iODR DR Detectors


Europa85L pairs seamlessly with Aspen's iODR Dr detectors, supporting both wireless and tethered iODR panel connections
