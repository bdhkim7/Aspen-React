Introducing Laica90

Bringing portable x-ray solution to Veterinary

Laica90 is aimed at delivering outstanding diagnostic precision in the palm of the vet's hands. In its small form factor, Laica90 is able to provide high quality x-ray imaging, ensuring clear visualization of anatomical details and allowing for accurate veterinary diagnostics.
