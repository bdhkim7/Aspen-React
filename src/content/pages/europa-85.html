Europa85

Introducing Europa

The control of a full X-ray unit with the flexibility of a portable system

We’ve designed Europa85 to give healthcare professionals the flexibility of a portable X-ray generator with the precision of a clinical room.

As the world’s first patented handheld X-ray with built-in image visualization, Europa85 delivers an unprecedented combination of immediacy, mobility, and clinical efficiency by eliminating the need for external monitors during image acquisition.
Europa 85

Highlights

Integrated 7" Diagnostic Display

Europa85 allows clinicians to view captured X-ray images instantly on its high-resolution built-in screen. This eliminates workflow delays and ensures immediate confirmation of diagnostic quality.

Advanced Imaging Quality

Adjustable 50-90 kV / 2-5 mA X-ray output and 0.04~1.3 sec exposure range. High- resolution imaging with 0.8 mm focal spot for precise diagnostics.

All-in-One Handheld X-ray System

With imaging controls, exposure, and display integrated into one device, Europa85 simplifies X-ray acquisition and reduces dependence on external equipment.

Clinical-Grade Imaging Quality

Engineered with advanced imaging technology, Europa85 delivers high-quality digital radiographs suitable for a broad spectrum of clinical applications.

Fast Workflow with Instant Verification

Immediate on-device viewing minimizes retakes, accelerates diagnosis, and supports faster clinical decision-making, especially in urgent or time-sensitive environments.
