Glass-Free Design

iODR1417-GF utilizes a flexible, unbreakable structure by removing carrier glass, reducing the risk of internal damage and enhancing durability. At its core, iODR1417-GF incorporates Cesium Iodide (CsI) Direct Deposition technology for improved robustness and performance.

High Image Quality

Boasts an impressive 86% Modulation Transfer Function (MTF) and 76% Detective Quantum Efficiency (DQE), setting an industry benchmark.

Provides exceptional diagnostic clarity with low radiation dose and advanced features, ensuring detailed anatomical visualization.

Durability & Environmental Resilience
Features robust mechanical parts and undergoes strict internal reliability testing for stable operation and low maintenance costs. Rated IP56 for water resistance and dust-proofing, making it suitable for harsh environments.
